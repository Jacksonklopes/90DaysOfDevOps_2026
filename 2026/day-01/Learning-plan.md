# 90 Days of DevOps – My Learning Plan

## My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud

DevOps is a way of working where development and operations teams work together to build, test, deploy, and maintain applications faster and more reliably.

**Cloud Engineering** involves designing, deploying, managing, and securing infrastructure and applications using cloud platforms such as AWS.

## Why I Am Starting

I come from a **non-IT background** and I want to build a career in the IT industry, specifically in **Cloud and DevOps**. I am starting this journey to gain practical skills, build real projects, and become job-ready.

## My 3 Goals for the Next 90 Days

1. **Build strong DevOps fundamentals** in Linux, networking, Git, Python, Docker, AWS, CI/CD, and Kubernetes.
2. **Build and deploy real-world projects** to gain practical hands-on experience and create a strong portfolio.
3. **Become job-ready for entry-level Cloud/DevOps roles** by improving my technical skills, preparing for interviews, and completing a production-grade Kubernetes deployment.

## 3 Core DevOps Skills I Want to Build

1. **Linux & Troubleshooting** – Become confident with Linux commands, system administration, networking, and troubleshooting.
2. **CI/CD & Automation** – Learn Git, GitHub, CI/CD pipelines, scripting, Docker, and automation.
3. **Cloud & Kubernetes** – Build strong AWS fundamentals and learn how to deploy, manage, and troubleshoot applications using Kubernetes.

## My Weekly Time Commitment

* **Daily:** 2 hours of focused learning and hands-on practice
* **Daily revision:** for revise previously learned concepts
* **Weekends:** 4 hours for deeper practice, projects, and revision

**Daily routine:** Learn → Practice → Revise → Document → Share

## My Commitment

For the next 90 days, I will focus on **consistency over perfection**. I will learn one concept at a time, practice it hands-on, revise what I have learned, and document my progress.

My goal is not just to complete 90 days. **My goal is to become job-ready for a Cloud/DevOps career.**
